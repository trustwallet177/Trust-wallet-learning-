What is a wallet?

A crypto wallet helps you manage the keys used to access digital assets on a blockchain. Your assets are recorded on the network, not stored inside an app like ordinary files.

⇄

Sending crypto safely

Confirm the coin, network, and recipient address. For a large transfer, consider a small test transaction first. Blockchain transfers are often irreversible.

⌁

Recovery phrases

A recovery phrase can give full control over a wallet. Keep it private and offline. Never type it into a website, send it to support, or share it with anyone.

STOP • CHECK • VERIFY

Know the warning signs

!

Fake support agents

Real support should never need your recovery phrase or private key to “fix” your wallet.

!

Guaranteed returns

Promises of guaranteed profit, doubled deposits, or risk-free returns are major red flags.

!

Unknown links and approvals

Do not connect your wallet to a site you have not verified or approve transactions you do not understand.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone ask me for my recovery phrase?

No. Treat it like the master key to your wallet. Never share it with a person, website, or support agent.

Can a crypto transfer be reversed?

Many blockchain transfers cannot be reversed. Verify every detail before confirming a transaction.
